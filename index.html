About Me

I am a Lecturer of Computer Science at Shenyang University of Chemical Technology. My research focuses on Medical Information Processing and High‑Performance Computing (HPC). By leveraging computational power and advanced algorithms to analyze complex physiological signals and large‑scale medical data, I aim to support clinicians with high-precision diagnostic tools and contribute to the development of efficient, next‑generation smart healthcare systems.

I received my Ph.D. in Computer Science from the Faculty of Information Technology (Műszaki Informatikai Kar) at the University of Pannonia (Pannon Egyetem).

Research Interests

  • Medical signal processing
  • Brain–computer interface
  • GPU computing
  • Machine learning
  • Computational neuroscience
