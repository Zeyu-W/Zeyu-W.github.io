About Me

I am an academic researcher focusing on educational data mining, competence evaluation, and scholarly writing. I work on data-driven approaches to understanding learning processes, and I mentor students and colleagues in research design, translation, and publication standards.

My current interests include feature selection for competence assessment, modular academic workflows, and reproducible interfaces for research communication.
