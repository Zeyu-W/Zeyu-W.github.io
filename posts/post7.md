<p style="text-align:justify;">
Another external project I worked on was an intelligent voice‑controlled cabinet. The requirement was offline recognition, and it shouldn't use machine‑learning‑based models, perhaps due to limited computing power or cost considerations. I developed the prototype and verified the algorithm, using Mel‑Frequency Cepstral Coefficients (MFCC) for speech feature extraction and matching.<br>
