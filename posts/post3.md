# IEEE-ISR International Conference, Shenyang

2018-08-26

<p style="text-align:justify;">
This was my first time attending an international academic conference—the IEEE International Conference on Intelligence and Safety for Robotics, jointly organized by the IEEE Robotics and Automation Society and the School of Electrical Engineering at Shenyang University of Technology. My senior labmate and I submitted a paper summarizing our previous work on a wearable EEG measurement system built with a Raspberry Pi. Fortunately, it was accepted.<br>
Most of the attendees were from China (a large portion were students from Shenyang University of Technology, many of whom were counting on this conference for graduation requirements). There were also many scholars from Japan. Their research was fascinating, and we had great conversations during the banquet. I even exchanged contact information with a young researcher from Tokyo Metropolitan University named 仓元昭季. We emailed a few times after the conference but eventually lost touch—I hope he’s doing well.
During the banquet, he also took me over to toast with the IEEE President, Professor Toshio Fukuda. It was quite an amusing moment because I had previously participated in a robotics competition (hosted annually by our School of Electrical Engineering) named after him, so we had a lively chat. Professor Fukuda’s election as IEEE President was closely connected to the votes from students at our school. I remember there was a period when the school encouraged all of us to register as IEEE student members—registration fees reimbursed—so that we could vote for him. He also seemed to serve as a visiting professor at many universities in China.<br>
I remember there was a keynote speech delivered by a young lady from Peking University. I no longer remember the exact topic, but her English was exceptionally fluent and her presentation was confident and engaging. If I remember correctly, she later won the Best Oral Presentation Award.
</p>
