# For Prof. Kozmann György :-(

2022-01-05

<p style="text-align:justify;">
My supervisor, Prof. György Kozmann, passed away in Budapest on January 5, 2022. RIP.<br>
Professor György Kozmann had been a full‑time lecturer at the University of Pannonia since 1998, and between 1999 and 2008 he served as the founding head of the Department of Information Systems. He later became Professor Emeritus. He played a decisive role in the establishment of the Faculty of Engineering Informatics. His name is associated with the introduction of courses in health informatics and the launch of related research at the University of Pannonia.<br>
Under his leadership, the Health Informatics Research and Development Center, the Brain Bioelectrical Imaging Research Laboratory, and the Cardiological Bioelectrical Imaging Research Laboratory were established. His professional interests included health information systems, the measurement and evaluation of bioelectrical phenomena, and their application for medical diagnostic purposes.
His colleagues and students will cherish his memory with deep respect.<br>
