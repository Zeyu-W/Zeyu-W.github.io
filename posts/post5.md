# A small plugin for my mom :-)

2020-03-12

<p style="text-align:justify;">
During the COVID-19 pandemic, my mom still has to deal with a lot of Excel spreadsheets every month, most of which involve tedious copy‑and‑paste tasks that leave her exhausted. To make things a little easier for her, I wrote a small Python plugin. To ensure it runs smoothly on Windows, I used the PyInstaller package, which bundles a Python script into a standalone .exe file.<br>
With this little plugin, all she needs to do is provide the paths of the source and target files, and it will automatically perform the series of repetitive operations she needs. Efficiency UP UP UP :-) The source code is here:<br>
[pluginForMom]([https://www.runoob.com](https://github.com/Zeyu-W/GuoYi-Co))
</p>
